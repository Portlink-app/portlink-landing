# User evidence and journeys

Direct user evidence in this task: "assess the entire landing page across all devices and register improvements and then do them". Earlier feedback: "I like this section" about the navy shared-call diagram, and a request to assess the actual app flow. These are the available user statements; no analytics, interviews or conversion rate evidence were supplied or invented.

Critical journeys:

1. Understand Portlink: opening, real product film, problem framing, seven product chapters, connected call.
2. Find your responsibility: shared-call handoffs, four role tabs and their evidence. Switching roles must not change the shared-call state.
3. Request the pilot: engagement choice, #access, organisation/contact details, operational details, back/edit. Stop before sending.
4. Commission software: homepage build action, /contact/, build selected, contact details, description of need, back/edit. Stop before sending.
5. Navigate from a small screen: open menu, choose a section, follow an inner page, return via its header/footer. Escape and keyboard focus must remain predictable.

Confirmed journey failures and fixes are tracked in BLUEPRINT.md. No claim of conversion uplift follows from these mechanical and visual checks.
